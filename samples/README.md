# Demo audio

For the submission video, download a short excerpt from a previous Nerdearla talk that you are allowed to use and select it through **Play audio file**. Audio is decoded in the browser; MP3, WAV, M4A and most video containers supported by Chrome work without conversion.

Do not commit third-party recordings unless their license permits redistribution. A microphone and the built-in `DEMO_MODE` are available for smoke tests.
